Build

Landscaping

Construction for gardens of every size, to our own designs or to someone else’s.

Our portfolio

Our work includes Chelsea Flower Show gardens and stands, roof terraces, shop and event installations, town house gardens, country gardens and holiday homes in France and Ibiza. We work on small gardens and on large construction projects.

Hard landscaping

The heavy work: excavation and groundworks, stone and brickwork, paving, carpentry, tree surgery and electrical installation.

Soft landscaping

The finer work: planting, lawns, flower arranging and horticulture.

Project management

We aim to deliver efficiently, within budget and with attention to detail, to minimise disruption, and to keep you informed throughout about anything that arises. Build quality is what lets a garden last for many years.

Overseas gardens

Overseas projects, in France or elsewhere in Europe, bring their own bureaucracy, language barriers and building rules. We have managed several, and offer a consultancy service for clients building a holiday-home garden who would like a UK-based firm involved.
