Services

Design, build and care

Design, build and care, offered together or separately. Some clients bring us a garden to design; others bring a design to build, or an established garden to keep.

  • 01Garden designA design that comes from the site and the people who will use it, drawn up in stages and carried through to the finished garden.
  • 02LandscapingConstruction for gardens of every size, to our own designs or to someone else’s.
  • 03Irrigation and water featuresSystems that keep a garden healthy in any weather, and water that gives it a focal point.
  • 04Garden lightingLighting that lets you use and see the garden after dark.
  • 05Living wallsPlanting on the vertical, for the spaces that have no room for borders.
  • 06Garden maintenanceRegular visits that keep an established garden in good condition all year, on a schedule that suits you.
