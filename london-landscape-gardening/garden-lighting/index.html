Landscaping

Garden lighting

Lighting that lets you use and see the garden after dark.

Seeing the garden differently

We design and install bespoke garden lighting that is safe and economical. It can pick out structure, planting and architectural features that are easy to miss in daylight, and give the garden a different character at night.

Good lighting also lengthens the time you can spend outside, turning the garden into an extra room for evenings, and lets you enjoy the view from indoors.

Control

Controls can link to your existing home lighting, a remote control, or a smartphone or tablet, and effects can be set to your taste.

Installation

Installation is carried out by qualified electricians to current industry standards, and certificates are supplied on completion. We specify fittings from suppliers including Hunza, Collingwood, Louis Poulsen and Oase.
