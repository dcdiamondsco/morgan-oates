Living walls

Planting on the vertical, for the spaces that have no room for borders.

What is a living wall?

A living wall, or green wall, is a wall partly or fully covered with plants rooted in soil, kept alive by a built-in watering system. It can be indoors or outdoors, free-standing or fixed to an existing wall, and in a wide range of sizes.

Living walls suit small spaces with architectural drawbacks: a plain brick wall becomes a wall of flowers and foliage.

Systems

We install Biotecture living walls, using good-quality products so that installations thrive and last.
