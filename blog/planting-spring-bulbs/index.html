Planting Spring Flowering Bulbs

Planting spring bulbs between September and December, before the first winter frost, gives a colourful spring. Good choices include daffodils, tulips, crocuses, hyacinths, lily of the valley, bluebells, puschkinia and fritillarias.

Bulbs are easy and need only well-drained soil and some sunshine. As a rule, plant each at two to three times its own height, about two bulb-widths apart, pointed end up. Refill the hole, break up large clumps and firm the soil gently.

In pots, choose a container to suit the bulbs, cover a large drainage hole with a crock, and fill with general-purpose compost mixed with a handful of horticultural grit. Potted bulbs need more attention: keep the compost moist, wrap pots against hard frost and cover with chicken wire to stop animals digging them up.
