Autumn Lawn Care

Autumn is a good time to help a lawn recover from summer heat and drought and prepare it for winter.

Clear fallen leaves from the grass at least weekly through autumn, with a rake or blower; left in place they form a damp, rotting layer that harms the grass. Summer foot traffic compacts soil, so aerate with a garden fork, making holes in the top few inches so rain and air reach the roots.

After the last summer mowing, feed with an autumn fertiliser of balanced nutrients and slow-release nitrogen, following the product’s directions.

Reseed bare patches in autumn: prick the surface with a fork, rake to a level seedbed, scatter seed evenly, cover with a thin layer of compost, firm with the back of a spade and water regularly.
