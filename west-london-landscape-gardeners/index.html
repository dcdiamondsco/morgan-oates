About

A small studio that designs, builds and looks after gardens

Morgan Oates is a landscape gardening and garden design company based in Cheltenham, working across the Cotswolds. It is a family team: Dan builds, Anna designs, and the two work side by side on every garden.

Director

Daniel Morgan Oates

Dan has been landscaping since his school days. He grew up in the Herefordshire countryside, trained at Pershore College, one of the country’s top horticultural colleges, and began his career in Herefordshire before moving to West London to work for a well-respected landscape company. A few years later he set up Morgan Oates.

Dan is the one who turns a design into a finished garden. He is an expert in interpreting plans for both hard and soft landscaping, and his eye for detail shows in the work. He has built award-winning, gold-medal gardens at the Chelsea Flower Show, and his commitment to customer service has earned him an excellent reputation with clients.

Garden Designer

Anna Pawlowska

Anna has loved gardens and nature since childhood. She grew up in the countryside, where her parents kept livestock and a small vegetable plot, and trained as a landscape architect, with a master’s degree from the University of Warmia and Mazury in Poland. Scholarships in Hungary, Portugal and Ukraine taught her how differently each culture gardens, and England’s garden tradition drew her here.

She joined Morgan Oates in 2013 as maintenance team leader, moved up to maintenance manager, and is now our garden designer, leading garden design and soft landscaping. She has worked on site on Chelsea Flower Show designs, and her design was chosen as one of five best show garden designs for APL at BBC Gardeners’ World Live 2019.

The crew

The people on site are friendly, experienced and professional. Site foremen oversee health and safety, keep sites tidy and keep noise down. Project management is about regular communication: if something comes up, you hear about it from us.

Design, build and aftercare

We take on garden design, the construction of designs by others, and maintenance of established gardens, together or separately. See the services, or get in touch.
