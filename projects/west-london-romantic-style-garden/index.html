West London Romantic Style Garden

A private, enclosed retreat for a young professional who works in the city centre and wanted to feel closer to nature. The concept rests on scent, birdsong and bees and butterflies, with a water feature for birds to bathe in.

The small site is used to its full potential. An existing lawn was being taken over by mind-your-own-business, so the design embraces it as a soft, cushioning ground cover, layered with shade-tolerant perennials and a few evergreen shrubs.
