Central London Garden

The entrance to a basement flat in central London, dark and shuttered, which limited what would grow. The design uses a faux living wall and trellis panels to cover the walls and a storage door.

Two tall planters of shade-loving evergreen climbers screen an unattractive staircase, spaced so the stairs stay accessible. A fern tree in a feature pot is reflected in a bespoke wooden-framed mirror outside the main bedroom. Furniture and a patterned carpet make the space feel lived in.
