Japanese Garden

Designed and built for a client who knows Japanese garden style and philosophy well. The brief was a calm, slightly secret retreat, tucked behind a curving walkway.

A bean-shaped pond is the centre of the garden, with a rockery waterfall visible from a gazebo built out over the water. That meant substantial groundwork before any planting began. Maples, rock and water do the rest.
