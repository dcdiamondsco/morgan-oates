Cheltenham · House and garden redevelopment

Cheltenham Town House and Garden

The redevelopment of a four-bedroom Arts and Crafts town house and its garden. Inside: new oak floors, rewiring and replumbing, relocated walls with structural steel, a bespoke kitchen and joinery, new staircase balustrades and new steel doors and windows.

Outside, a working garden for a keen gardener: a garden office, a silver birch woodland walk with low grasses, raised oak sleeper beds for fruit and vegetables, brick-edged hogging paths, a composting and working area, full irrigation to front and rear and a mood-changing lighting system.
